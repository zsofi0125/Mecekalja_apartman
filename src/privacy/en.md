The purpose of this Privacy Policy is to set out the data protection and data processing principles applied during the operation of the mecsekaljaapartman.hu informational website (hereinafter: the Website), in accordance with Regulation (EU) 2016/679 of the European Parliament and of the Council (GDPR) and Hungarian Act CXII of 2011 on the Right of Informational Self-Determination and on Freedom of Information (Infotv.).

The up-to-date identification details, registered address, tax number, and direct contact details of the Data Controller are available in the Imprint (Impresszum) section of the Website. The Data Controller does not employ a Data Protection Officer (DPO).

## 3. Details of Data Processing Activities

### 3.1. Direct Phone and E-mail Inquiries
- Scope of processed data: Data voluntarily provided by the visitor (name, e-mail address, phone number, and the content of the message or phone conversation).
- Purpose of processing: Answering inquiries regarding the accommodation and providing requested information.
- Legal basis: Voluntary consent of the data subject (Article 6(1)(a) GDPR) or taking steps prior to entering into a contract (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR).
- Retention period: Up to 1 year following the closure of the inquiry, provided no booking is made.

### 3.2. Technical Operation and Server Logging
- Scope of processed data: Visitor's IP address, browser and operating system type, timestamp of the visit, and visited subpages.
- Purpose of processing: Ensuring the secure and stable operation of the Website, and preventing or mitigating malicious cyberattacks (e.g., DDoS).
- Legal basis: Legitimate interest of the Data Controller (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR).
- Retention period: Security log files are retained for a maximum of 30 days.

### 3.3. Cookies and Web Analytics (Google Analytics)
The Website uses cookies to enhance user experience and analyze visitor traffic. A cookie is a small text file stored by your browser on your device.
- Strictly Necessary (Technical) Cookies: Essential for the basic operation and security of the Website (Cloudflare). Legal basis: legitimate interest (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR).
- Statistical Cookies (Google Analytics): Using Google Analytics, we collect anonymized statistical data on how visitors interact with the Website (e.g., page views, session duration, traffic sources). Google Analytics cookies are activated only based on the visitor's prior, voluntary consent (Article 6(1)(a) GDPR) given via the cookie consent banner. Consent may be withdrawn at any time via browser settings or by adjusting cookie preferences.

### 3.4. Embedded Third-Party Content (Google Maps and Google Fonts)
- Embedded Google Maps: To display the location of the accommodation, the Website features an interactive Google Map (iframe embed loaded from [https://www.google.com](https://www.google.com) and [https://maps.google.com](https://maps.google.com)). When loading the map, Google may process technical data (such as the visitor's IP address and browser details) and set its own cookies. The embedded map is not linked to a Google Business Profile account.
- Google Fonts: To ensure consistent typography, the Website loads font files from external servers ([https://fonts.googleapis.com](https://fonts.googleapis.com) and [https://fonts.gstatic.com](https://fonts.gstatic.com)). During this request, the visitor's IP address is transmitted to Google's servers; however, Google Fonts does not set marketing or tracking cookies.
- Legal basis: Legitimate interest of the Data Controller in providing a modern user interface and accurate location information (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR), and visitor consent regarding map-related cookies (Article 6(1)(a) GDPR).

## 7. Rights of Data Subjects

Visitors (data subjects) have the following rights regarding the processing of their personal data:
- Right of access: Request information about what personal data is being processed.
- Right to rectification: Request the correction of inaccurate personal data.
- Right to erasure ("right to be forgotten"): Request the deletion of personal data where there is no longer a legal basis for processing.
- Right to restriction of processing: Request restricted processing in case of contested accuracy or unlawful processing.
- Right to data portability: Receive personal data processed automatically based on consent in a machine-readable format.
- Right to object: Object to data processing based on legitimate interests.
- Right to withdraw consent: Withdraw consent (e.g., for statistical cookies) at any time without justification.
